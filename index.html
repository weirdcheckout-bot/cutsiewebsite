Integer operations

Multiply and divide positive and negative numbers.

Remember: same signs → positive; different signs → negative.
Worked example

(−6) × 4 = −24 because the signs differ. (−24) ÷ (−6) = 4 because the signs match.

🔎

Construct expressions

Turn words into mathematical expressions.

Remember: “less than” reverses the order; “twice” means ×2.
🧩

Factoring

Rewrite an expression as a product.

Remember: find the greatest common factor (GCF) first.
🔢

Place value & rounding

Know each digit's value and round accurately.

Remember: 0–4 stays, 5–9 rounds up.
